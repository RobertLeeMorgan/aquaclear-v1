    content: |
      Reed beds are an important wetland habitat, but without regular management they can become increasingly dense, reducing open water and restricting water movement. Cutting and clearing helps maintain a healthy balance between reed growth and open water, supporting wildlife while preserving the wider function of the reed bed.

      The Truxor's lightweight design and manoeuvrability allow our operators to work across deep and shallow water, soft ground, and dense reed beds where conventional machinery can struggle to operate. Cut reed can be collected and transported to a nearby accessible location for processing as required.
