    eyebrow: How We Manage Removed Silt
    title: Silt Containment & Dewatering
    description: Aquaclear uses different containment and dewatering methods depending on the site, available space, ground conditions, and quantity of material being removed.
    items:
      - title: Dewatering Bags
        content: |
          Dewatering bags are suitable for areas of flat, even ground where there is sufficient space to deploy them. Custom-made bags can be produced in sizes up to 5m × 12m and are filled with silt-laden water before being left to drain naturally through their woven geotextile material.

          Multiple bags can be used simultaneously, allowing some to drain while others are being filled. Half-size bags measure 5m × 6m, while full-size bags measure 5m × 12m.
        media:
          type: image
          src: ../../assets/images/services/siltbags.jpg
          alt: Dewatering bags used for silt removal
      - title: Geotextile Barriers
        content: |
          Geotextile barriers, also known as permeable membranes, are useful on uneven or sloping terrain where dewatering bags cannot easily be deployed.

          The barriers can be installed with a sloped surface behind which silt-laden water is pumped. As the water drains through the permeable material, the silt is trapped and consolidated for later removal.
        media:
          type: image
          src: ../../assets/images/services/siltbarrier.jpg
          alt: Geotextile barrier used for silt containment
      - title: Soil Bunds
        content: |
          Soil bunds can be formed using excavators near the water source to create a containment area for pumped silt and water. The trapped water gradually drains through the soil, leaving the silt to dry naturally.

          Once dry, the silt can be removed, reused, or left to green over, depending on the requirements of the site.
        media:
          type: image
          src: ../../assets/images/services/siltbunds.webp
          alt: Soil bund used for silt containment
