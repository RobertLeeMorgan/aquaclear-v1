aboutAquaclear:
  eyebrow: Who We Are
  title: Over 20 Years of Experience Across the UK
  content: |
    Aquaclear Water Management provides specialist contracting services for waterways, wetlands and aquatic environments throughout the UK.

    We have worked with environmental organisations, local councils, private clients and other organisations to maintain and restore watercourses of all shapes and sizes, from large lakes and reedbeds to canals, ponds and other sensitive environments.

    Our work covers aquatic vegetation control, reed bed management, silt removal, dredging and other waterway maintenance. We combine practical experience with specialist equipment to carry out work safely, efficiently and with minimal impact on the surrounding environment.
