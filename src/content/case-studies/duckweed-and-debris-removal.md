      title: Duckweed on UK Waterways
      content: Duckweed is a common floating aquatic plant found across UK ponds,
        lakes and slow-moving waterways. Under favourable conditions it can
        spread rapidly, forming dense mats across the water surface that reduce
        light penetration and suppress submerged plants. On top of being
        unsightly, heavy coverage can contribute to low dissolved oxygen levels,
        particularly as plant material dies and decomposes, placing additional
        stress on fish and other aquatic life. Dense growth can also restrict
        areas of open water, interfere with recreational use and give ponds an
        unsightly, stagnant appearance. While duckweed is a natural and valuable
        part of many freshwater habitats, excessive growth can upset the balance
        of a waterbody and require active management.
