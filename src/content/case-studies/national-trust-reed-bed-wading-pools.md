    - title: Managing Dense Reed Growth
      content: |
        At a National Trust site, Aquaclear was brought in to carry out reed management within an established wetland area.

        Over time, dense reed growth can reduce the amount of open water available within a reed bed, limiting the range of habitats available to wading birds and other wetland wildlife.

        The first stage of the work was to clear selected areas of reed and open up sections of the existing habitat, providing space for further work and creating a more varied wetland environment.

    - title: Creating Wading Pools
      content: |
        Following the reed clearance, areas within the reed bed were excavated to create a series of shallow wading pools.

        The pools introduced areas of open, shallow water between the remaining reed beds, creating a mixture of open water, wet margins and established vegetation across the site.

        This variation is particularly valuable in wetland environments, providing feeding areas where wading birds can access shallow water and exposed margins.
      media:
        type: image
        src: ../../assets/images/projects/reed2.webp
        alt: Shallow wading pools created within the reed bed
    - title: Creating a More Diverse Wetland
      content: |
        The completed work created a more varied structure across the reed bed, combining retained areas of established reeds with newly opened pools and shallow wetland margins.

        These different habitats provide opportunities for a wider range of wildlife while maintaining the character and ecological value of the existing reed bed.

        The project demonstrates how targeted reed management and small-scale excavation can be used to improve the diversity and functionality of established wetland habitats.
