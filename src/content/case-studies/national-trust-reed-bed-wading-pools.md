    - title: Opening Areas of Reed Bed
      content: >-
        Aquaclear regularly carries out reed bed management across nature
        reserves and established wetlands. Sometimes we are asked to create
        additional areas of open water within dense reed beds alongside regular
        maintenance works.


        Creating shallow wading pools within dense reed beds introduces valuable areas of open water and increases habitat diversity. These pools provide accessible feeding and foraging areas for wading birds and other wetland species, while the surrounding reeds continue to offer shelter, nesting habitat and cover. The combination of shallow water, open margins and established vegetation creates a more varied wetland environment capable of supporting a wider range of wildlife.

    - title: Creating Wading Pools With a Truxor
      content: >-
        In some reed beds, simple cutting and clearance is enough to expose
        shallow areas of open water and create suitable wading pools. Where
        greater depth or a more defined pool is required, the Truxor can be
        equipped with a range of attachments to carry out further excavation
        once the reeds have been removed.


        Clamshell buckets and excavator arms can be used to remove reed rhizomes, accumulated organic material and silt, allowing the depth and profile of individual pools to be carefully shaped. Where conditions are suitable, silt pumps can also be used to remove softer sediments and create or maintain areas of shallow open water.


        The versatility of the Truxor allows cutting, clearance and excavation to be carried out from within the wetland itself, making it particularly well suited to targeted habitat creation in areas where access for conventional land-based machinery may be difficult or undesirable.
